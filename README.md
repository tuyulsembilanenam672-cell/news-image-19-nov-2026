A deterministic newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn pixel by pixel with Node's standard
library only — no network, no fonts, no dependencies.

## The day in seven stories

- **GTA VI begins its global rollout** — after a 13-year wait, gamers enter Vice City.
- **US and Iran trade fire again** — Tehran hits a US base in Kuwait and Bahrain after US strikes on Qeshm Island.
- **Leaders meet in Shenzhen** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold trilateral talks at the APEC summit.
- **EU plans a EUR 45bn Ukraine loan** — Brussels plans a loan to fund Kyiv, repaid once Russia pays reparations.
- **Cyclone Akvan nears India** — the storm is set to cross the coast between Machilipatnam and Puri.
- **Voyager 1 reaches one light-day** — NASA believes its probe has reached one light-day from Earth after nearly 50 years.
- **Jeffries nominated for Speaker** — Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries, who would be the first Black House Speaker.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` draws a 1024×1024 PNG and writes it to `entry.png` in the
output directory. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
